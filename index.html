Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions. Message us on WhatsApp for anything else.

How do I place an order?

Message us on WhatsApp with the furniture you're looking for, your room size and budget. We share designs and photos, confirm the final price and delivery date, then share payment details.

Can you make custom furniture?

Yes. We make furniture to your measurements, design and choice of wood, fabric and finish. Drawings and pricing are confirmed over chat before production begins.

Do you deliver and install?

Yes. Our team delivers to your home or office and handles assembly and installation, so your furniture is ready to use.

What are the warranty, delivery and return terms?

Warranty, delivery charges, timelines and return terms depend on the product and order. These are confirmed with our team before you order.
